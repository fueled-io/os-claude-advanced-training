# Graduation

**Every participant has a repo someone else has successfully run.**

1. [ ] **Fix your gaps.** For each question your partner had to ask, add the answer to your repo: in CLAUDE.md, `WORKFLOW.md`, a step file, or the golden example README. Read each diff, commit, push. Your pull request picks up the changes.
2. [ ] **Rerun the handoff test.** Ask your partner (or anyone) to run `gh pr checkout` with your pull request number, and run it again from the golden input.
   *Done when* they get the golden output without asking you anything.
3. [ ] **Check whether your workflow needs more than claude.ai.** If it needs a pinned model or effort setting, or should run on its own without someone at the keyboard, flag it to Sean and we'll scope it.

You've graduated when step 2 passes.
