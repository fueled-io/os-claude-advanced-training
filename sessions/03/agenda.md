# Session 3: Break the work into steps

**60 minutes:** 30 minutes of teaching, then 30 minutes hands-on.

**Premise:** Small, inspectable steps beat one big prompt. Walkthrough of Annual Rev Reporting as the worked example.

## Teaching (30 minutes)

- **The shape:** inputs, numbered steps, outputs, each step its own file with a stated definition of done. Template: [`templates/workflow.template.md`](../../templates/workflow.template.md).
- **Why small steps win:** checkpoints you can inspect, failures you can localize, steps you can rerun without redoing everything.
- **Plan first:** ask Claude to write the plan first, then execute one step at a time with review between steps.
- **Three kinds of file:** raw inputs (never committed), derived files (regenerated by the steps), and historical outputs (committed, never overwritten by a re-run). Reference: [`docs/file-types.md`](../../docs/file-types.md).
- **Audit trails:** writing audit trails when manipulating data. Reference: [`docs/audit-trails.md`](../../docs/audit-trails.md).
- **Re-runs:** updated routines shouldn't unintentionally break historical data on re-run.

## Hands-on (30 minutes)

Decompose the workflow from Session 2 into numbered steps with a done-check on each. Label every file in the repo as raw, derived, or historical, and put the raw ones in .gitignore.

Step by step: [`exercise.md`](exercise.md).
