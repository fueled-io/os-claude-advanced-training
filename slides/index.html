Opensend · Training series

We're All Software Developers Now

Four 60-minute sessions, one per week, on working with Claude. Each session is 30 minutes of teaching and 30 minutes hands-on, with a small assignment that feeds the next session. Everyone finishes with a repo a teammate can clone and run.

  • Session 1

    Don't ship slop.

    Judgment about what's worth producing, the sender's contract, the pre-send checklist. Ends with everyone signed in to GitHub.

  • Session 2

    GitHub as your backend

    The five verbs, README and CLAUDE.md, memories vs. context vs. skills, and why the repo is the source of truth.

  • Session 3

    Break the work into steps

    Inputs, numbered steps, outputs. Raw, derived, and historical files. Audit trails, and re-runs that don't break history.

  • Session 4

    Same result, every time, for everyone

    Procedures as files, golden examples, the handoff test, and generative vs. decision models.

Present: open a session, then use the arrow keys (or Space) to move through slides and F for full screen. Print: your browser's Print dialog gives one slide per page. Source of truth for every slide: AGENDA.md and sessions/ in this repo.
